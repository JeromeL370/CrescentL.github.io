Introducing Spatiotemporal Topological Modeling Aids in Visual Decoding

Hongyi Liu, Xvxvan Xie, Yuchen Guo, Youyong Kong†

IEEE International Conference on Systems, Man, and Cybernetics (SMC), 2026. Accepted CCF-C

A functional-topology framework for fMRI-based visual reconstruction. Cross-sample sliding-window averaging produces stable patch-level representations; correlation-based graphs capture functional connectivity; and the resulting graph features are fused with fine-grained masked features to condition a latent diffusion model. The method improves top-1 classification accuracy by roughly 8% and yields sharper reconstructions on GOD and BOLD5000.

  • Brain Decoding
  • Graph Neural Networks
  • Latent Diffusion
  • fMRI

MM-SSP: A Self-Evolving Paradigm for Multimodal Search Agents

Feb – Jun 2026

Research Intern · Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences

Addressed the scarcity of high-quality training trajectories for multimodal search agents with a two-role “setter–solver” framework. The setter retrieves iteratively from image evidence, constructs intermediate question–answer pairs, and composes a final question; the solver answers and returns feedback. This enables a zero-supervision, self-involving agentic RL pipeline, improving search-based answer accuracy by approximately 20%.

Contributed to image–text index construction, setter-rule design, model deployment, and training.

  • Agentic RL
  • Multimodal Search
  • LLM Agents

ClaimLens-MA: A Multi-Agent Fact-Verification System

Apr – May 2026

Personal Project · LangChain & LangGraph

Built an eight-agent verification system to counter confirmation bias and unauditable conclusions in single-agent fact checking. Conditional routing realizes a closed loop of argument, challenge, evidence completion, and re-argumentation. Claims are classified as supported, refuted, mixed, or insufficiently evidenced, and every verdict remains traceable to its source evidence.

Designed the agent architecture and built, deployed, and debugged the end-to-end system.

  • Multi-Agent Systems
  • Fact Verification
  • LangGraph

Knowledge-Graph-Enhanced RAG with Semantic Subgraph Retrieval

Dec 2024 – Jun 2025

National Undergraduate Innovation Project · Southeast University

Motivated by GraphRAG, we constructed cross-document knowledge graphs to address knowledge fragmentation and limited interpretability in conventional RAG for complex question answering. An optimized semantic McSplit subgraph-matching module retrieves structurally relevant evidence to guide the language model, allowing the system to exploit structured information while making its reasoning more transparent.

Implemented the semantic McSplit subgraph-matching component and evaluated the full RAG pipeline.

  • Knowledge Graphs
  • Retrieval-Augmented Generation
  • Subgraph Matching
